A 1:1 Pixel-Perfect Clone of Apple Music UI/UX designed exclusively for playing locally downloaded music files on your Android device with fluid spring physics.

---

### ✨ Features & Capabilities

* **Local Music Only (Zero Dummy Data)**: Clean onboarding interface ready to scan device storage (`/Music`, `/Download`, SD card).
* **Wide Codec Support**: Plays `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.flac`, `.wav`, `.aac`, and `.ogg` files offline.
* **Metadata & Cover Art Extraction**: Automatically parses ID3v1/ID3v2 tags and embedded APIC album artwork.
* **Dynamic Fluid Mesh Gradient**: Multi-orb animated ambient gradient background that samples album art palette in real-time.
* **Apple Micro-Interactions**: Squircle artwork scales down on pause and blooms up on play with realistic spring bounce.
* **Time-Synced Karaoke Lyrics**: Real-time auto-scrolling lyrics with glowing active line and tap-to-seek.
* **Android MediaSession**: Complete lock-screen playback controls, notification shade controls, and Bluetooth headphone/car audio support.
* **Persistent Storage**: Retains songs, playlists, favorites, and play counts offline in IndexedDB.
